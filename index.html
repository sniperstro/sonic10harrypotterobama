🎒 The Backpack

The origins of #Sonic10HarryPotterObama trace back to February 2010, when a photograph of a gloriously wrong bootleg backpack first surfaced online.

By February 4, 2010 it had been posted to Best Week Ever; a month later, on March 9, 2010, GamesBeat crowned it "The World's Most Confusing Backpack." It was a flea-market knockoff — a Chinese bootleg by the earliest accounts, later lovingly rediscovered in a little Dutch shop — and it instantly became the most cursed specimen in the entire bootleg-knockoff canon.

Printed across the bag: a mangled, hand-drawn Sonic the Hedgehog with the number 10 on his chest — flanked by HARRY POTTER in dripping red and OBAMA in rainbow, wrapped in roses. A crossover nobody asked for, and nobody could ever un-see.

"Why does Sonic have the number 10 on his chest? So many questions… so few answers."— GamesBeat, 2010

"SHPO10" rode alongside the image from the very beginning. The key distinction: Sonic10HarryPotterObama is the name of the original 2010 meme — the backpack, the source — long before any chart or any ticker ever existed. Everything else is downstream of the bag.
